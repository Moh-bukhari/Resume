# LinkedIn connection notes and direct messages

Use a verified profile whose current company, function, and identity match the
intended recipient. Profile access may be limited; record what was actually
visible and do not claim to have read inaccessible material. These are drafts.

Choose one connection note or one direct message. Recount the final note after
substitutions: target **at most 200 characters including spaces**, and recheck the
account's current interface limit because platform limits vary. Keep direct
messages to about 100 words or fewer. No email address is needed for this channel.

## Connection note: before applying

```text
Hi [Name], I'm exploring [Company]'s [Role] opening. I build talent workflows at Tamara and expect to graduate in May 2027. Could we connect?
```

## Connection note: after a confirmed application

```text
Hi [Name], I applied for [Role] at [Company]. I built talent CRM workflows at Tamara and expect to graduate in May 2027. Could we connect?
```

Long names or titles can push either note over the target. Shorten the company
or role reference accurately and remove detail before sending; never truncate a
person's name or invent an abbreviation. Both notes have one ask: connect.

## Direct message: before applying

```text
Hi [First name], the [role title] posting's focus on [sourced responsibility] connects to my work at Tamara. I mapped recruiting and leadership workflows into a Greenhouse-integrated talent CRM around 700K+ historical applicant records.

I expect to graduate from UC Berkeley in May 2027 and prefer full-time work afterward. Would you be open to a brief conversation about the role and timing?

Mohammed
```

## Direct message: after a confirmed application

```text
Hi [First name], I applied for [role title] on [confirmed date]. Its focus on [sourced responsibility] connects to my work at Tamara, where I built a Greenhouse-integrated CRM linking recruiting and talent-brand workflows.

I expect to graduate in May 2027. Would a brief conversation about the team's priorities and start timing be useful?

Mohammed
```

Use the pre-application variant if status is unknown. Do not claim a referral,
prior meeting, accepted connection request, immediate availability, or hiring
authority without evidence. Do not add guessed personal contact information.

If the event is confirmed but its exact date is unknown, omit the date phrase
from the message rather than inventing it. Keep date metadata unknown. Exact
follow-up timing remains pending until the actual sent date is known.
