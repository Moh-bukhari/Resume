# Cover letter

Use after analyzing the full JD and selecting supported evidence. Copy this
file into the application folder, then tailor the example to the role. Target
250–350 words for the final body, excluding contact details, date, and signature.
Keep 3–4 readable paragraphs. A cover letter can add reasoning and context; it
should not repeat the entire resume or make unsupported company diagnoses.

Use a verified recipient's name, or the appropriate recruiting/team salutation
when no person is known. A professional email is needed only when delivering by
email, not for an application portal. Do not invent an address or postal location.
The draft itself neither submits an application nor sends a message.

Replace the default evidence when a different bank entry fits better; update
claim/source IDs accordingly. Preserve 700K+ as historical applicants and 500 as
priority targets, not conversions. Do not turn a company need into an established
internal problem or imply immediate availability, a completed degree, work
authorization, a referral, or application submission. Mention earlier work only
as openness, with exact availability still to confirm. Count words after filling
placeholders and cut repetition before delivering.
