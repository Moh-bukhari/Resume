# Before applying: hiring-manager email

Use when a verified professional contact is relevant to a current role and a short
conversation would clarify the work or timing. Check the posting first; do not
delay a deadline or skip its required application route. Drafting does not send.

Copy this file into the application's `letters/` folder. Fill the metadata, then
replace every bracketed placeholder in the message. Keep the final body at
90–150 words, excluding subject and signature. One sourced detail and one
relevant example are enough; select the example for the job and avoid adding
a list of achievements. This repository's short outreach target takes priority
over the upstream cold-email skill's longer examples.

## Subject options — choose one

- `[Role title] — Mohammed Bukhari`
- `Quick introduction — Mohammed Bukhari`

## Copyable draft

```text
Hi [First name],

The [role title] posting's emphasis on [specific, sourced responsibility] caught my attention because it connects to work I've done at Tamara. I mapped talent-brand, recruiting, and COO workflows into a shared CRM built around 700K+ historical applicant records, with Greenhouse integration and engagement tracking.

For [Company], I'd be interested in applying that experience to [concrete need stated in the posting]. The overlap is translating several teams' needs into one usable system, with connected handoffs and a shared view of the work.

I expect to graduate from UC Berkeley in May 2027 and prefer a full-time start afterward. Would a brief conversation about the team's priorities and timing make sense?

Mohammed Bukhari
H7@berkeley.edu
https://mobuk.me
```

Before finalizing, verify the recipient still works on this function and the job
is current. Use the 700K+ figure only with its historical-applicant scope. If
using different evidence, update `claim_ids` and their bank source IDs. Do not
invent a shared connection, company news, a prior conversation, immediate
availability, or a recipient email address. A missing work email leaves an email
draft without a destination; use a verified LinkedIn profile if appropriate.
