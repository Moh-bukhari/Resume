# Referral request

Use with a real contact or a professional who has explicitly invited referral
requests. Otherwise start with the ordinary introduction template and a smaller
ask. A shared university or company alone does not establish a relationship.
Do not imply the recipient has agreed to refer Mohammed or can bypass hiring.

Keep the final body at 90–150 words. The relationship sentence must describe a
confirmed interaction; remove it if it cannot be substantiated. Confirm whether
the employer requires referral before application, but do not invent a policy.

## Subject options — choose one for email

- `[Company] [role title] — referral question`
- `Your perspective on [role title]`

## Copyable draft

```text
Hi [First name],

[One sentence recalling a confirmed interaction or the recipient's invitation to request referrals.] I'm considering [role title] at [Company], particularly its focus on [specific, sourced responsibility].

At Tamara, I mapped talent-brand, recruiting, and COO workflows into a shared CRM with Greenhouse integration. Working around 700K+ historical applicant records meant connecting existing recruiting information with event engagement and follow-up. That seems relevant to the role's need for [supported connection]. I expect to graduate from UC Berkeley in May 2027 and prefer a full-time start afterward.

If the background and timing seem suitable, would you feel comfortable referring me for this opening? My work is summarized at https://mobuk.me.

Mohammed Bukhari
H7@berkeley.edu
```

The single ask is permission for a referral. Do not add a meeting request and
resume-review request as additional asks. If already applied, add that fact only
with the confirmed date and record it in metadata. Mention an attached resume
only if the reviewed artifact is actually included. For LinkedIn, remove the
subject and shorten to the channel's practical limits.
