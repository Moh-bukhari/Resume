## Role summary (from the posting)

Be Retell AI's in-house expert in driving successful customer deployments of its
voice-AI platform: partner with customers to understand goals, design and build
scalable voice agents, and launch them into production. Blend business acumen,
technical fluency, and customer empathy; work cross-functionally with Sales,
Product, Engineering, and Customer Success.

## Key responsibilities (condensed from the posting)

- Lead end-to-end customer deployments: discovery, use-case design, build,
  production launch; own delivery timelines, stakeholder communication, and
  risk management.
- Partner with customer stakeholders to define success criteria; translate
  business goals into AI workflows.
- Become a subject-matter expert in Retell's platform, voice-AI best
  practices, and LLM prompting; advise customers and internal teams.
- Conduct discovery sessions and business-process reviews; design
  conversational workflows and scalable deployment architectures.
- Build prototypes and validate use cases; advise on ROI and measurable
  business outcomes.
- Act as a trusted advisor to executives on AI transformation; build ROI
  models and business cases.
- Partner with Sales in pre-sales: scoping, solution design, presentations.
- Coordinate across Product, Engineering, and Customer Success; translate
  customer feedback into product recommendations.
- Write clear requirements, deployment documentation, and internal playbooks.

## "You might thrive if you" (from the posting)

- Communicate with credibility across executives, operators, and technical teams.
- Have strong product intuition for customer workflows and pain points.
- Balance technical detail with business strategy and outcomes.
- Set structure in ambiguity.
- Take end-to-end ownership and bias to action.
- Have run complex, multi-stakeholder projects.
- Background in deployment, implementation, solutions consulting, product
  management, forward-deployed engineering, strategic consulting, or a similar
  customer-facing technical discipline.
- Can write requirements, implementation plans, or PRDs.
- Have done ROI analysis, value-based scoping, or business-case development.
- Are excited about AI, LLMs, and voice interfaces; comfortable in technical
  conversations without needing to be a full-time engineer.

## Interview process

Talent screen (15 min); Functional interview (45 min); Hiring manager (30 min);
Final interviews with Product, Engineering and GTM (3 hrs).
