## Method applied to every message (cold-outreach skills + humanizer)

- **Structure, always in this order:** (1) one real, recent, verifiable signal
  from the person's own words or work; (2) the consequence of that signal for
  their seat — the inference is what makes the message unmistakably his;
  (3) exactly ONE proof point matched to that consequence; (4) exactly ONE
  small ask answerable in a sentence (a read of the application, a name, a
  quick word). No meeting ask, no calendar link on first contact.
- **One proof point, never a resume.** Tamara = pre-ATS recruiting CRM,
  Greenhouse API, OpenAI natural-language search over 700K+ historical
  applicant records, adopted by TA + leadership including the COO; SCET =
  AI + HubSpot API workflow, 100K+ leads → 500 priority targets; Sitters =
  voice AI, Claude + ElevenLabs, 1st place Anthropic hackathon; AynX founder.
  Facts kept exact.
- **Altitude calibrated:** founders get outcomes, managers get process,
  practitioners get their day-to-day task.
- **Budgets enforced:** email body 60–90 words (sign-off excluded), LinkedIn
  connection note ≤280 characters, DM under 100 words. Counts verified by
  script after writing; see the verification note at the end of this file.
- **Rejection test on every message:** if it could go to anyone else at the
  company by changing the name, it is not ready. Every live message passed.
- **Banned:** compliments, self-introduction first, "I hope this finds you
  well," "I came across your profile," mail-merge phrasing, "quick question"
  as a subject, "let me know if you have 15 minutes."
- **Humanizer last** (skills/blader/humanizer/SKILL.md): no not-X-but-Y
  contrasts, no forced triads, no em-dash overuse, no stock AI words, no
  inflated claims, no sales language.
- **Profile-research rule (standing workflow, from 2026-10-23):** every message
  must reference one niche, specific detail researched from that person's
  LinkedIn. Generic hooks are banned. A message without a profile-specific
  hook does not go out — except Weijia Yu, where no profile exists and no
  hook was invented.
- People already 1st-degree get a DM draft, not a connection note.
- People with a pending LinkedIn invite get email only — never re-send an invite.
- One person, one live message: where the same person appears in both packets,
  the plan says which message is live and which is a HOLD/second touch. Never
  send two messages to the same person in the same week.
- All emails say he applied Friday 2026-10-23 (past tense).
