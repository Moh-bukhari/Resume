## Role summary (from the posting)

Founders Initiatives is a cross-functional team that works directly with
Retell's founders on the company's highest-priority opportunities and
challenges, building from first principles across product, engineering, GTM,
operations, and AI systems. This role designs and implements internal and
external AI systems that power how Retell hires, sells, operates, and scales —
production-grade AI workflows, automations, and agent systems with direct
impact on company operations and customer experience. It sits at the
intersection of applied AI, full-stack engineering, and startup operations.

## Key responsibilities (condensed from the posting)

- HR & Recruiting AI systems: build systems that extract structured signals
  from resumes (skills, experience, founder traits, domain expertise); develop
  lead-scoring models to predict interview success and role fit; design AI
  recruiter workflows for candidate screening and qualification; automate
  personalized outbound email and candidate engagement workflows.
- Security & compliance automation: automation for customer security
  workflows (BAAs, DPAs, DocuSign, contract review flows); automated security
  questionnaire and compliance response generation; internal tooling for
  enterprise readiness and audit processes.
- Operational infrastructure tools: accounts-receivable automation and
  finance email workflows; automated enterprise customer onboarding and
  account setup; internal systems that reduce manual operational overhead.
- 3rd-party integrations & platform work: integrate CRMs such as Salesforce,
  HubSpot, and GoHighLevel; build workflow automation with tools like Zapier,
  n8n, and Make; design LLM-powered systems using OpenAI, Gemini, Claude, and
  other models; own API-based integrations across the company.
- GTM engineering & growth automation: HubSpot and Lark automations; lifecycle
  automation for customer engagement and retention; website personalization
  and ad content generation systems; email personalization and outbound
  automation pipelines at scale.

## "You might thrive if you" (from the posting)

- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or a closely
  related technical field.
- 2+ years of professional software engineering experience.
- 1+ year of startup experience; comfortable with ambiguity and fast iteration.
- Think like a founder and operate with high ownership.
- Strong software engineering skills (backend, full-stack, or systems).
- Hands-on experience building with LLMs or AI agents in production.
- Comfortable working across product, engineering, and operations.
- Move fast, iterate quickly, prefer building systems over narrowly defined
  tasks, and care about real-world impact over theoretical work.

## Interview process

Intro call (15–20 min); Technical phone interview — system design & project
deep dive (45 min); Second tech screen — tools & automation (45 min); Final:
coding and technical round (90 min), cross-functional interview (30 min),
past project deep dive (30 min).
