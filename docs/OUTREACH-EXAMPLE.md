# Example: a role-specific introduction

Illustrative unsent draft using the previously user-supplied Mercor Growth
Associate requirements. The posting has not been checked live and no recipient
was researched for this example. Update from the current bank and actual role
before reuse. Application status is not assumed.

```text
Subject: Growth Associate — Mohammed Bukhari

Hi [First name],

Mercor's Growth Associate role caught my attention because it combines finding the right people with building the systems that keep those relationships moving.

At Tamara, I used Sales Navigator for targeted event outreach and added 500+ qualified early-career candidates to our pipeline. I also built the Greenhouse-integrated CRM our talent teams and leadership used to track candidate touchpoints and follow-ups. At Berkeley SCET, I built an AI/HubSpot workflow narrowing 100K+ leads to 500 priority targets.

I expect to graduate from Berkeley in May 2027 and am exploring full-time roles afterward, with some flexibility earlier. Would my background be relevant to your next hiring cycle?

Mohammed
https://mobuk.me
```

The opening comes from the role, without invented company praise or a personal
connection. Candidate evidence: `TAM-05`, `TAM-06`, `TAM-09`, `TAM-15`, `TAM-25`,
`SCP-06`, and bank graduation/availability facts. Counts keep their original
scope. There is one question about fit/timing, with no assumed application,
attachment, recipient address, prior meeting, or immediate start.

For a confirmed post-application version, name the actual application, then use
relevant evidence. See [the letter guide](LETTERS.md) for stages and
[contact research](CONTACT-RESEARCH.md) to replace the recipient placeholder.
