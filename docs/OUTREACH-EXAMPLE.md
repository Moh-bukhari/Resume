## Before applying: ask a real timing question

```text
Subject: Growth Associate — Mohammed Bukhari

Hi [First name],

Mercor's Growth Associate role combines sourcing specialists with building lasting talent pipelines, work I've been doing at Tamara.

I used Sales Navigator to build targeted event-outreach funnels and organize personalized invitations through the COO's LinkedIn. Across summer channels, I added 500+ qualified early-career candidates to our warm pipeline. I also built the Greenhouse-integrated CRM our talent teams and leadership used to track candidate touchpoints and follow-ups.

I graduate from Berkeley in May 2027 and am open to starting earlier. Could that timing work for your Growth Associate hiring?

Mohammed
https://mobuk.me
```

Use only if the application is not yet submitted and the timing question is
useful. Apply through the normal route when ready; a cold reply is not a
prerequisite. Candidate facts: `TAM-05`, `TAM-06`, `TAM-09`, `TAM-15`, `TAM-25`,
`TAM-26`, and bank graduation/availability. The role's sourcing requirements
support the opening; there is no invented personal connection or company news.
