# Humanizing skills selected for this application system

The required pair is **Blader Humanizer v3.1.0** followed by **Corey Haines Copy
Editing**, adapted through [Professional Writing Review](../skills/professional-writing-review/SKILL.md).
Blader improves naturalness and removes formulaic phrasing. Corey's clarity,
proof, and specificity checks keep the final wording strong. A final comparison
protects the bank's facts, scope, technical language, and company alignment.

This is a recommendation for Mohammed's resumes and professional messages,
based on inspected instructions and their fit. No comparative hiring-outcome
benchmark establishes a universal best skill.

## Why this combination preserves strong wording

- Draft from the bank and the company-fit brief first, so the editor has a
  concrete argument to sharpen.
- Blader removes boilerplate, canned enthusiasm, unnecessary contrast, and
  repetitive structure while checking meaning.
- Corey checks that the rewritten text is clear, credible, specific, and useful
  to this reader. Strong supported verbs and technical mechanisms remain.
- The local final pass compares all facts, qualifiers, attribution, stage,
  and company relevance against the sources. Resume fragments and useful
  keywords are legitimate; naturalness does not require casualness.

For example, preserve “implemented” when it accurately describes implementation.
Do not change “700K+ historical applicant records” to “700K+ users,” remove
“shared” from project payments, or turn 500 selected targets into customers.
No invented anecdotes, personal passion, humor, typos, or forced sentence quotas
are permitted just to make a passage feel more human.

The selected upstream files remain unchanged. Local rules override generic
suggestions to invent opinions/reactions, remove every dash, apply rigid
formatting rules, or add marketing emotion/guarantees. We use editorial patterns
to improve prose; they do not establish who wrote it or guarantee detector results.

## Installed files and usage

- [Blader original](../skills/blader/humanizer/SKILL.md),
  [MIT license](../skills/blader/LICENSE), and
  [pinned provenance](../skills/blader/UPSTREAM.md).
- [Corey Copy Editing](../skills/marketingskills/copy-editing/SKILL.md),
  its references, [MIT license](../skills/marketingskills/LICENSE), and
  [existing provenance](../skills/marketingskills/UPSTREAM.md).
- [Professional Writing Review](../skills/professional-writing-review/SKILL.md):
  the required sequence and format-specific protections.

Shared AI instructions, local resume/letter/application skills, and all packet
templates route through the review. Notes and message metadata start `pending`;
the AI records review only after checking the actual final text. This is a
portable Markdown procedure with no new account, runtime service, or automatic
sending. Existing submitted/sent versions remain historical records.
