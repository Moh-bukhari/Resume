# Choose and write the right letter

See [a worked introduction](OUTREACH-EXAMPLE.md) for the target style.

The writing methods come from Corey Haines' published
[Cold Email](../skills/marketingskills/cold-email/SKILL.md) and
[Copy Editing](../skills/marketingskills/copy-editing/SKILL.md) skills. Read the
original files and relevant references. This guide supplies job stage,
candidate facts, and delivery context; it does not replace their methodology.

Use [application-letter-writer](../skills/application-letter-writer/SKILL.md).
For a complete packet, use [application-workflow](../skills/application-workflow/SKILL.md).
Read the actual job description, selected resume, bank, contact research, and
confirmed application/thread events before writing.

## Choose the stage

| Situation | Recommended draft |
| --- | --- |
| Open role with clear fit | Prepare/apply through the required route; a short introduction can accompany or follow it. Do not wait for a cold reply and miss the application. |
| Unclear scope/start timing, no listed opening, or a real referral connection | Pre-application question or referral request with one specific ask. |
| Application confirmed for this exact role | Post-application note naming the role and one or two strongest evidence points. |
| No reply to a confirmed sent message | One useful follow-up, usually after about 5–7 business days; follow employer/user timelines first. |
| Interview completed | A short thank-you within roughly one business day, based on the actual conversation. |
| Scheduling, offer, terms, acceptance/decline, withdrawal, references | Event-specific reply using the confirmed thread, terms, and user decision. |

These are editable suggestions, not scheduled actions. A draft starts no
follow-up timer. A reply changes the plan: address it instead of continuing
an old sequence. Usually one primary contact and one fallback are enough.
Avoid simultaneous pitches to everyone on the team.

When an event is confirmed but its exact date is unknown, omit the date phrase:
“I applied for [role]” or “Thank you for our conversation.” Do not invent dates
or block a useful draft. Keep an exact follow-up date pending until its actual
sent date is known. A user-provided contact or actual thread is valid contact
provenance; no invented public-source URL or repeated contact lookup is needed.

A template is a starting point. Choose the evidence for the role rather than
retaining the same Tamara paragraph in every letter. Produce the needed draft
and useful optional variants; there is no reason to send all eight templates.

## Writing standard

1. **A real connection to the work.** Use a sourced requirement, product
   decision, or hiring priority. If there is no researched personal hook, use
   the actual JD instead of invented praise.
2. **A compact relevant example.** One or two bank facts that explain fit.
   Keep historical applicant scope, selected-target scope, shared payments,
   and personal/team credit accurate. Do not paste the whole resume.
3. **One clear ask.** A role/timing question, a short conversation, or a
   specific next step. Do not combine requests for a referral, resume review,
   meeting, and introduction in one message.

Write warmly and directly. Use a concrete subject, natural language, and short
paragraphs. Avoid inflated flattery, “perfect fit,” a generic mission paragraph,
and a compulsory apology about experience. Do not invent a prior relationship,
referral, product usage, interview topic, application date, or attachment.

For a recruiter, make the exact role, fit, and start timing easy to understand.
For a likely team leader, connect their work to a relevant system/problem you
have handled. A founder is useful only when the company/role makes that natural.

Expected graduation is May 2027; the user prefers afterward and is open to
earlier work. Mention timing when it matters, but do not invent immediate
availability, location, visa status, or a commitment to a start date.

## Evidence and output

Keep `claim_ids`, company/recipient sources, dates, chosen stage/channel,
word/character counts, and uncertainty outside the copyable letter. Unknown
contact details stay placeholders or blank records. Published email and
provider verification remain separate evidence types.

Save drafts under `applications/<company>-<role>/letters/` and record the
recommended next step in `outreach-plan.md`. Preserve actual sent versions
when a later user-authorized action occurs. Do not mark drafting as sending.

Deliver copyable subject/body, why that recipient and stage were chosen,
contact evidence, and important gaps. Include complete LaTeX when producing
a resume. Plain Markdown/text is the default for letters; if a formal letter
PDF is requested, provide a complete document and check its layout too.

Offer, withdrawal, and negotiation drafts need confirmed terms and the user's
actual decision. The seven historical job offers in the bank are not current
competing-offer leverage.
