## The combination adapted to Mohammed

1. **Ground the application.** Read the JD, factual bank, application state,
   and existing packet. Account for expected May 2027 graduation; ask only
   missing questions that affect the draft.
2. **Find one relevant contact and one fallback.** Apply Exa's role/company
   patterns with current team evidence. Save the exact found LinkedIn URL.
   Distinguish a likely function leader from a confirmed hiring manager.
3. **Research one useful connection.** Apply Anthropic's sourced brief and
   inference labels. A JD requirement is a valid hook when no person-specific
   fact exists. Use Firecrawl only when deeper context improves the draft.
4. **Find email when needed.** Prefer published professional details; optionally
   use Apify. Separate published addresses, provider results, inferred patterns,
   and deliverability statuses. A guessed pattern never becomes verified.
5. **Choose one or two relevant bank facts.** For talent/growth roles, consider
   Tamara's 500+ qualified candidates and Sales Navigator/COO invitation funnel;
   for systems/deployment, the CRM around 700K+ historical applicant records
   and stakeholder workflows; for growth automation, SCET's 100K+ leads filtered
   to 500 targets. Preserve each metric's scope.
6. **Write for the actual stage.** Combine Anthropic's relevance/evidence/ask
   structure with Corey's concise writing and editing. Use local templates
   for pre/post-application, referrals, LinkedIn, follow-ups, thank-yous, and
   formal cover letters. Aim for 100–120 words, flexible up to 150; channel and purpose
   take priority over rigid upstream counts or sales calls to action.
7. **Check and save.** Verify voice, specificity, evidence, recipient, and one
   clear ask. Save source notes, bank IDs, copyable drafts, and timing outside
   the message body. Follow-ups start from a confirmed sent event. Do not miss
   an application deadline while waiting for a cold reply.
