## What this copy provides

Account Research supplies evidence-backed company/person research, explicit
inference labels, and concrete relevance hooks. Draft Outreach supplies
history-aware cold/warm intent selection and a concise relevance/value/ask
structure. Their original examples describe B2B selling; local application
workflows adapt those methods to Mohammed's job search and factual bank.

## Access and scope

Upstream supports optional enrichment, CRM, email, and sales-engagement
connectors and a files-only fallback. Copying these Markdown files installs no
tools, accounts, credentials, paid services, or live contact database. Use
available search tools and user-provided material and disclose gaps.

The original skill's connector, sending, scheduling, CRM-writing, and rendering
instructions describe its host product. They do not add authorization to send
messages, submit applications, enroll contacts in sequences, or change external
records. Root `AGENTS.md`, the user's request, and the active assistant's
instructions govern this repository's scope. Sales ICP criteria and calls to
action are examples, not requirements for job applications.
