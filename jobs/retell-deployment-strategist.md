Source: user-pasted job description. Role requirements excerpt; live status

Location: San Francisco Bay Area / Redwood City, on-site, full-time.

## Role

Strategic, customer-facing work at the intersection of technology, product,
and execution. Lead AI voice-agent implementations from discovery through
production launch, help customers redesign processes, define requirements,
scope solutions, drive adoption, and realize measurable business outcomes.
Partner with Product, Engineering, Sales, and Customer Success.

## Responsibilities supplied by the user

- Own end-to-end customer deployments, execution, stakeholder communication,
  risk management, and delivery.
- Understand business goals, gather requirements, and translate them into
  scalable AI-powered workflows.
- Learn Retell's platform, voice-AI practices, and LLM prompting.
- Design conversational workflows, automation strategies, and deployment
  architectures delivering measurable business outcomes.
- Build prototypes, validate use cases, and explain implementation tradeoffs
  and optimization opportunities.
- Advise executives and operational leaders on AI opportunities.
- Lead discovery, business-process reviews, and strategic-planning sessions.
- Build ROI models and business cases to prioritize automation initiatives.
- Support Sales in pre-sales, scoping, and customer presentations.
- Coordinate Product, Engineering, and Customer Success to unblock delivery.
- Communicate progress, risks, recommendations, and next steps to technical
  and nontechnical stakeholders.
- Translate customer feedback into product recommendations.
- Develop repeatable deployment playbooks and best practices.
